Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what personal information 365 Travel ("we", "us") collects through 365travel.co, why we collect it, and what you can do about it. 365 Travel is based in the United Kingdom and this policy is written with UK GDPR in mind.

What we collect

At present this site collects information only when you give it to us directly:

  • Contact form — your name, email address, phone number (if given), the topic you select and your message.
  • Newsletter sign-up — your email address.

We do not currently use cookies, analytics, or any tracking technology on this site. The search widget and payment options shown are illustrative — no booking or payment data is collected or processed through them yet.

How we use it

We use the information you submit to respond to your enquiry, action a booking-related request, or send you the occasional deal or update if you've subscribed. We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for anything you haven't asked us to.

Who we share it with

Form submissions are delivered to us via FormSubmit, a third-party form processing service, which handles the message in transit. We don't otherwise share your personal information with third parties, except where required by law.

As this site integrates with hotel and travel suppliers (such as RateHawk) and payment processors (including crypto payment providers), information necessary to complete a booking or payment — such as your name, travel dates, and payment details — will be shared with those providers at the point you make a booking. We'll update this policy before that functionality goes live.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and newsletter data only as long as necessary to respond to you or while you remain subscribed. You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see below).

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, and to withdraw consent (such as unsubscribing from the newsletter) at any time. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data: hello@365travel.co.

This is a general-purpose policy and should be reviewed by a qualified solicitor before the site processes real bookings or payments.
