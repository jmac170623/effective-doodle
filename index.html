Questions

Good to know before you book

Is the site live for real bookings yet?

Not quite — search, pricing and the payment options shown are a preview of what we're building. Right now the fastest way to actually book is to get in touch directly and our team will arrange it for you.

What payment methods will you accept?

Major cards, bank transfer, and leading cryptocurrencies including Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT, USD Coin, Solana, Litecoin and Dogecoin. You'll choose whichever suits you at checkout.

Is paying with crypto safe?

Yes — payments are handled through established, secure payment processors. Because crypto values can move between the moment you pay and when it settles, we'll always make the exchange rate used clear before you confirm.

Can I cancel or change a booking?

Most stays include free cancellation up to a set date — this will be shown clearly on each booking before you pay. For changes, our support team can help directly.

Do you charge booking fees?

No — the price you're quoted is the price you pay. No surprise fees added at checkout.

How do I get in touch?

Use the contact form below, email hello@365travel.co, or call us — we're available 365 days a year.
