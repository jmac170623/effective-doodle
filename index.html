About us

A private travel office, not a booking site

Travel Exchange 365 exists for clients who don't have time to plan and shouldn't have to compare websites. Our travel architects have lived and worked in the places they book, and our partner network — hoteliers, private aviation desks, yacht owners, guides — answers to us directly, so nothing is left to a call centre.

  • One point of contact — from the first call to the return transfer home.
  • No public rate cards — every proposal is negotiated and priced for you.
  • Real people, always reachable — a named concierge, 365 days a year.
